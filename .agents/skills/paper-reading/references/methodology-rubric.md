# Methodology Rubric

Use only the dimensions relevant to the paper.

## Problem

- Is the research question precise and testable?
- Is the motivation supported by a real gap or need?
- Are the scope and assumptions explicit?

## Method

- Are inputs, outputs, variables, and objectives defined?
- Is the method logically connected to the question?
- Are assumptions plausible for the target setting?
- Are important implementation details available?

## Experiments

- Are datasets representative and sufficiently described?
- Are baselines strong, current, and fairly configured?
- Do metrics measure the stated objective?
- Are ablations, controls, or sensitivity analyses adequate?
- Are uncertainty, variance, or statistical tests reported when needed?

## Validity

- What threatens internal validity?
- What threatens external validity or transferability?
- What limits reproducibility?
- Which conclusions are justified, overstated, or unsupported?
