# Evidence Standard

## Claim ledger

Record important claims in this form:

| Claim | Type | Evidence | Location | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Concise claim | paper fact / inference / evaluation | equation, result, table, figure, or passage | page/section/figure/table | confirmed / check |

## Support levels

- **Direct**：论文明确陈述，且可以定位到原文。
- **Empirical**：由实验、数据或表格直接支持，但不一定证明因果关系。
- **Inferential**：根据多个事实推导出的解释，应标记为推断。
- **Speculative**：合理但尚未被论文证据支持，只能作为待研究假设。

## Minimum checks

Before writing a strong conclusion, check:

1. Is the source location available?
2. Does the evidence measure the same concept as the claim?
3. Are the baseline and comparison conditions fair?
4. Could another factor explain the result?
5. Does the conclusion exceed the population, data, or task studied?

If any answer is unclear, weaken the wording and mark the item for verification.
